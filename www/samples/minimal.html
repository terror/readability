This is the smallest document that still reads like an article: a title and a few paragraphs of text. Edit it to see how the extracted content changes.

Content shorter than the minimum text length is still returned, but the parser retries with progressively looser heuristics first. Try lowering the minimum text length in the parser options to skip those retries.

Elements such as navigation, footers and sidebars are usually removed, while paragraphs with plenty of text and few links are kept.
